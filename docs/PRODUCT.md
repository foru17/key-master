# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Self-hosted gateway owners auditing access and resolving approvals from a desktop or phone.

## Product Purpose

Make access decisions, their scope, and their expiry explicit. Help owners investigate requests and revoke credentials quickly.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, restrained. The visual contract is [DESIGN.md](DESIGN.md).

## Anti-references

Legacy administration grids, decorative dashboards, hidden security state, and subscription content previews.

## Design Principles

- Surface pending decisions before configuration.
- Keep audit context available while acting.
- Show secrets once and make revocation clear.
- Preserve density on desktop and readability on phones.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Chinese and English, keyboard navigation, reduced motion, responsive layout, light/dark/system themes and text contrast of at least 4.5:1.
